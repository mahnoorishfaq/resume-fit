How the score works

Two things are measured and combined.

Skill coverage (75% of the score). Both documents are scanned for known skills using a dictionary of aliases, so "sklearn", "scikit-learn" and "scikit learn" all count as the same thing. Coverage is the share of the job's skills that appear in your resume.

Wording similarity (25%). TF-IDF cosine similarity between the two documents. This catches overlap in how things are described, not just skill names.

What it cannot do

It cannot tell a skill you used from one you merely listed. Someone who stuffs a skills section with keywords will score well. Treat the number as a prompt to look closer, not a verdict on whether to apply.
Files are processed in memory and never saved. Scores are a guide, not a gate.
